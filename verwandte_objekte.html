Verein für Geschichte- und Altertumskunde e.V. Frankfurt-Höchst

Verwandte Objekte

Machine-suggested related items from the society's museum-digital catalog, generated for volunteer review. For each query object, the pipeline embeds the catalog text and lists the most similar published objects.

Similarity is the cosine similarity of multilingual sentence embeddings built from each object's title, description, type, material, tags, and related places — higher means the catalog texts are more alike. Suggestions below the 0.35 threshold are not shown.

These are suggestions only: nothing is written back to museum-digital. Cluster groupings come from HDBSCAN over the full 34-object index.
